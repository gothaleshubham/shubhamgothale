About Shubham

I'm Shubham Praful Gothale, an Enterprise AI Architect based in Pune, India building intelligent systems that go from research to production at scale.

Over 7+ years, I've worked across healthcare, legal tech, and conversational AI. from fine-tuning open-source LLMs to architecting multi-agent orchestration platforms. My career spans roles at TRDDC, Quantiphi, Zycus, Gupshup, and Simplify Healthcare, where I've led AI teams, built production AI pipelines, and delivered systems that impact real users.

Today, I specialize in Agentic AI, Multi-Agent Systems, and RAG-powered knowledge platforms, leveraging LangChain, LangGraph, and CrewAI to turn complex business workflows into autonomous, intelligent experiences.

I hold an M.Tech from IIT Kharagpur and combine deep research expertise with the ability to ship production-ready AI at scale.
